# Research resources

Start with the [completed CEO speech and R&D example](../examples/completed-nbm/README.md). It follows one application through all four stages, reports the delivered findings, and distinguishes them from the runnable fixture. Its [aggregate results and field guide](../examples/completed-nbm/results/README.md) explain the three included evidence files.

| Research task | Guide | Questions to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Represent narratives | [Topic workflow](../workflows/01-topics.md) | What corpus, observation unit, topic model, and row/column identities define the inputs? |
| Assess relevance | [Screening workflow](../workflows/02-screen-topics.md) | What does relevance mean, what evidence may readers use, and how are disagreement and missingness retained? |
| Construct the proxy | [Prediction workflow](../workflows/03-build-proxy.md) | What behavioral target, features, timing, sample, transformations, and cross-fitting scheme define the score? |
| Interpret the evidence | [Assessment workflow](../workflows/04-assess-proxy.md) | Which comparisons support the intended interpretation, and which competing explanations remain? |

For a new application, record the construct definition, unit, period, narrative sources, behavioral target, intended use, and assessment criteria before execution. A CEO-risk specification does not automatically transfer to another construct.

The [workflow guide](../WORKFLOW.md) covers execution and handoff. The [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) identifies further reproduction, provenance, validation, and reuse work. The included evidence is a compact illustration of completed findings; it does not replace the study inputs needed for a full empirical run.
