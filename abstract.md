Organizational microfoundations are generally hard to observe and quantify. Behavioral proxies face the problem of attribution, while narrative proxies face the problem of authenticity. We develop an AI-empowered framework to integrate behavioral and narrative cues for the same microfoundational feature into a synthesized proxy. The framework combines unsupervised topic modeling, LLM agents, and supervised machine learning in a four-step workflow: characterizing the corpus, topic decontextualization, synthesizing the proxies, and validation. A demonstration focuses on CEOs’ risk preference, using conference-call narratives to predict R&D intensity. Grouped out-of-fold predictions serve as the synthesized proxy. The synthesized proxies predict held-out R&D intensity and remain associated with dividend payment after controlling for the narrative-only proxy and actual R&D, supporting feasibility and incremental explanatory value. Convergent construct validity remains to be established. The framework offers a scalable and inspectable toolkit, allowing AI judgments to function as explicit, versioned, and falsifiable measurement inputs for organizational research.
