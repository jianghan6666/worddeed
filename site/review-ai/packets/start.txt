# Understand the workflow

WordDeed proposes a measurement workflow for organizational research when neither what people say nor what organizations do reveals an individual characteristic directly. First, topic discovery represents a narrative corpus. Second, researchers define the construct and AI readers judge topic relevance, retaining reasons and disagreement. Third, supervised models learn how selected narrative features predict an observed behavioral anchor; held-out predictions become a candidate synthesized proxy. Fourth, researchers assess that proxy against external outcomes and competing measures. The intended contribution is an inspectable connection between semantic judgments, measurement and evaluation. The CEO example connects conference-call language, R&D intensity and dividend payment to illustrate feasibility, not to establish a psychological ground truth. A connected prototype and saved-judgment replay exist; fresh API screening still lacks required released inputs. Predictive fit, agreement and execution success each leave construct validity unresolved. Another application needs its own construct, data, anchor and validation choices.
