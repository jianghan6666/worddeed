## What the demonstration contributes

Saved results show that language predicts part of the variation in R&D intensity. Higher synthesized scores are associated with lower odds of paying a dividend after the included controls. This provides a path from inspectable judgments to a behaviorally calibrated measure and a separate empirical application. It supports feasibility and possible usefulness while exposing assumptions for scrutiny. [S2 §§3–4; S3 prediction metrics and downstream model records]

The submitted appendix also reports that the association persists after including a direct narrative benchmark. Its eight A13 models are reported evidence outside the existing twenty-five-model replay. Their separate replay materials remain to be integrated. This is a meaningful distinction between a submitted result and its operational verification. [S2 §4.4/A13; S3 connected reproduction receipt]

## What remains to learn

The case establishes neither psychological ground truth nor a causal effect. Shared circumstances can influence speech and investment. The synthesized score is only weakly related to the direct narrative benchmark, so narrative convergence remains limited. One topic selection, adopted partly after preliminary predictive comparisons, supplies the reported score; agreement is not validated accuracy. Further work must assess interpretation, sensitivity to screening choices and other constructs. [S2 §§2.4–3.6/4.5, Table A15]
