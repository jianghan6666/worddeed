Measuring organizational
microfoundations

Organizational microfoundations—the cognitions, beliefs, and traits of individual members, as well as their collection and integration—can steer firms’ strategic decisions, operations, and performance. These latent cognitive and psychological properties are generally hard to observe and quantify.

Both streams of microfoundational proxies face potential measurement errors. Behavioral proxies face the problem of attribution; narrative proxies can be challenged by the problem of authenticity.

The proposed workflow

Researchers define the construct, inputs and evaluation.

Basic workflow

We integrate three core AI techniques—unsupervised machine learning (topic modeling), LLM agents, and supervised machine learning (gradient boosting and regularized regression)—to develop a scalable workflow.

  1. 01Characterizing the Corpus
  2. 02Topic decontextualization
  3. 03Synthesize the narrative and behavioral proxies
  4. 04Validate the synthesized proxy

Characterizing the Corpus

We use unsupervised topic discovery to establish the thematic structure of the corpus. In the proposed design, topic discovery is applied to the corpus in its entirety and remains microfoundation-agnostic.

What a researcher can inspect

  • The corpus and topic-model specification
  • Topic descriptors and source passages
  • The link from each observation to its features

An application’s actual training population and representation choices must be stated.

Topic decontextualization

The task here is one of selection and interpretation—establishing which topics constitute evidence about the focal feature rather than about the contextual specificities facing a firm. Agents first classify topic descriptors, then review common source passages within their own histories. An unweighted two-of-three majority determines selection.

What a researcher can inspect

  • The construct definition and reading instructions
  • Passages cited when judgments changed
  • Agreement, disagreement and unresolved cases

Agreement shows where readers concur. Accuracy needs an independent reference.

Synthesize the narrative and behavioral proxies

The narrative proxy is used to predict an established behavioral proxy for the focal microfoundational feature. Predictions for observations excluded from supervised fitting serve as the candidate synthesized proxy.

What a researcher can inspect

  • The behavioral target and its transformations
  • Selected features, fitted models and held-out groups
  • Predictions and a separate signed residual

The difference between observed and predicted behavior needs a separate interpretation. It does not by itself show deception.

Validate the synthesized proxy

Step 4 strives to gauge the synthesized proxy in terms of both convergent validity and incremental validity. The first asks whether it is consistent with alternative measurements; the second asks whether it affords extra explanatory power beyond the narrative proxy and the behavioral proxy together.

What a researcher can inspect

  • Agreement among measures
  • Added information in a separate empirical test
  • Alternative explanations and sensitivity checks

Predictive fit and significant associations alone do not establish construct validity.

These stages describe the proposed design. Each application must justify its measurement choices and test the resulting proxy.

Why AI matters

A scalable and
inspectable toolkit

  1. AI judgments as measurement inputs

    The framework aims to allow AI judgments to function as explicit, versioned, and falsifiable measurement inputs rather than opaque intermediaries.

  2. A design template

    If the framework works, it offers a design template that confronts a central empirical challenge: inherent measurement error. Comparing alternative topic selections through later stages remains development work.

  3. Other microfoundational features

    Further work will examine more microfoundational features at various levels and different sources of behavioral and narrative cues. Each application needs its own measurement argument, compatible inputs and validation.

Prototype and development

Key risks and
future work

What exists

The prototype connects stage tools and records their inputs, outputs and saved state. The CEO application supplies archived AI judgments, learned scores and downstream estimates. A small synthetic demo shows how outputs pass between stages.

Inspect what can be checked or run →

What comes next

LLM judgments may prove unstable across model versions and prompting configurations. We plan to address this risk through version pinning and a complete validation record covering model versions, prompts, reasoning effort, and source context. Independent assessment and tests of shared firm influences remain necessary.
