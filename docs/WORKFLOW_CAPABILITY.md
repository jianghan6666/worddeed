# Make narrative judgments part of a testable measure

Organizational researchers often infer beliefs or preferences from what people say or what organizations do. Both can mislead: speech may be strategic, and the same action may reflect different opportunities or constraints. WordDeed proposes a way to connect these imperfect sources and examine what their relationship can tell us.

The workflow represents recurring themes in a corpus, uses independent AI readers to assess their relevance to a researcher-defined construct, learns how the retained narrative features predict a behavioral indicator, and assesses the resulting proxy in a downstream empirical application. Its contribution is to make semantic judgments explicit measurement inputs: researchers can inspect the evidence and disagreement behind a selection, then test how alternative selections affect the measure. That broader sensitivity analysis remains development work.

The prototype connects these stages through saved artifacts, computational adapters and reuse of completed stages. It includes archived reader judgments, deterministic voting, grouped prediction and downstream assessment code; a model coordinator is optional. The CEO case illustrates the connection from speech to investment prediction and a separate corporate decision. It demonstrates feasibility, while the interpretation as a psychological attribute still requires validation. Another application needs its own construct definition, behavioral anchor, validation criteria and compatible adapters.

**Inspect it progressively.** Follow a topic from evidence and reader reasons to the selected-feature handoff. Run the small offline example to inspect stage connections and saved state; its replies and numbers are invented. Recalculate the archived votes, or follow the documented empirical route for numerical reproduction with compatible inputs and software. These are different checks, and none alone establishes construct validity.

**Current execution boundary.** Saved judgments from the earlier AI chat study can be replayed without new inference; this does not rerun that study through the newer API adapter. The adapter for one fresh three-reader analysis is implemented, but the reviewed replay archive lacks 21 required input files and fails its preflight before any model call. It requires a complete verified input package and a separately recorded live run. The numerical reproduction starts from saved topic representation and judgments; it does not regenerate topic discovery or sentiment.
