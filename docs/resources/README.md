## Evidence in the presentation

- The released CEO application contains twelve two-stage screening trials, one adopted 321-topic selection, grouped prediction scores for 29,506 firm-years, and the original 25-model dividend package. The connected reproduction reuses saved representation and reader judgments, then refits prediction and estimates those 25 models.
- The final appendix, Section 4.4 and Table A13, additionally reports eight models that include both a narrative proxy and actual R&D controls. All eight synthesized-proxy coefficients remain negative and significant at the 1% level. Narrative-proxy p-values range from 0.357 to 0.582. These additional models are reported by the authors; they are not covered by the existing connected reproduction receipt. Their input definition, code, and outputs remain to be integrated into that route.
- The proposal reports a political-ideology trial using political donations. This repository currently packages the CEO application; no political-ideology results or reproduction are claimed here.
- Re-estimating all topic selections, averaging their predictions, selection-informed regularization, independent human-reference assessment, and broader construct validation are proposed work.

The presentation uses the appendix’s distinctions among reader agreement, predictive fit, conditional associations, and construct validity. The final documents remain unchanged, including broader interpretations in the proposal. [Reviewer guide](../REVIEWER_GUIDE.md) · [Research film](../index.html#film).

## Input and method guides

Start with the [completed CEO speech and R&D example](../../applications/ceo-risk-preference/README.md). It follows one application through all four stages, reports the delivered findings, and distinguishes them from the runnable fixture. Its [aggregate results and field guide](../../applications/ceo-risk-preference/summary/README.md) explain the three included evidence files.

| Research task | Guide | Questions to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Represent narratives | [Topic workflow](../workflows/01-topics.md) | What corpus, observation unit, topic model, and row/column identities define the inputs? |
| Assess relevance | [Screening workflow](../workflows/02-screen-topics.md) | What does relevance mean, what evidence may readers use, and how are disagreement and missingness retained? |
| Construct the proxy | [Prediction workflow](../workflows/03-build-proxy.md) | What behavioral target, features, timing, sample, transformations, and cross-fitting scheme define the score? |
| Interpret the evidence | [Assessment workflow](../workflows/04-assess-proxy.md) | Which comparisons support the intended interpretation, and which competing explanations remain? |

For a new application, record the construct definition, unit, period, narrative sources, behavioral target, intended use, and assessment criteria before execution. A CEO-risk specification does not automatically transfer to another construct.

The [workflow guide](../WORKFLOW.md) covers execution and handoff. The [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) identifies further reproduction, provenance, validation, and reuse work. The [full results](../../applications/ceo-risk-preference/RESULTS.md) provide the complete findings; the [replication guide](../../applications/ceo-risk-preference/REPLICATION.md) supplies access to the inventoried study inputs and execution instructions.
