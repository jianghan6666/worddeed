# Represent the narrative

## Inputs

Study catalogue, topic densities, observation metadata, and model identity.

## Procedure

Bind the ordered topics to the study observations. Reuse compatible saved densities or an explicitly configured inference implementation. Check dimensions, keys, and available model fingerprints; record what remains delivery-declared.

## Output

Catalogue and density locations, identities, ordering checks, and limitations.

Use the [runtime guide](../agent_system/empirical/README.md) for commands. The [completed example](../examples/ceo-risk-preference/README.md) illustrates the delivered method; the [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) identifies further assessment.
